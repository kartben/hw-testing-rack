# PCBA pre-order review (pcba-design-skills v1.0.0)

Review of the five boards on `main` at `99d875a`, done on 2026-10-19 before the JLCPCB order.  It
uses Keitark's pcba-design-skills v1.0.0 as
checklists, with their gates (`PASS` / `USER_REVIEW` / `BLOCKED`) and their scripts.  The skills
used:
- **design-and-review-circuit:** power, protection, operating states and ratings.
- **pcb-layout-review:** placement, planes, DRC and DFM.
- **qualify-pcba-sourcing:** MPN, package and footprint coherence, stock.
- **release-pcba-fabrication:** Gerber, BOM and CPL consistency with the board.
- **operate-jlcpcb-order:** only its CPL placement review and `audit_cpl_geometry.py`, none of the
  browser, quote or order steps.

Nothing was uploaded to JLC and no board was changed: the fixes below go to the board owners.

**Tools.**
- `kicad-cli` 10.0.7, locally and read-only; the files were generated by 10.0.6 in Docker.
- The suite's `audit_cpl_geometry.py`, `validate_sourcing_lock.py`, `build_release_manifest.py` and
  `validate_release_manifest.py`.
- JLC's own footprints, the EasyEDA component data of all 71 LCSC parts.
- LCSC/JLC stock checked on 2026-10-19 at 21:15 UTC.
- Datasheets, cited per finding.

The per-board records are local and git-ignored, in `.pcba-workflow/<board>/`:
- `circuit-review.md`, `layout-review.json`;
- `sourcing-lock.csv` and its validation;
- `release-manifest.json` and its validation;
- `cpl-audit/`;
- `erc.json`, `drc.json`;
- the adapters, in `.pcba-workflow/tools/`.

Severity: **critical** = wrong assembly or a damaged board · **major** = fails a rating, a datasheet
requirement or the board's own budget · **minor** = works, below what a reviewer accepts ·
**info** = noted.  Owners: **Électronique** = the electronics session (hub, node, DATA port,
camera pod), **DATA ×4** = the DATA ×4 session.

## CPL placement against JLC's own footprints

**How it was audited.**
- JLC places its own EasyEDA footprint at each CPL point.  The audit fits JLC's pads onto the
  KiCad pads for every placed part, `audit_cpl_geometry.py` with the adapter in
  `.pcba-workflow/tools/cpl_adapter.py`.  It then compares the result with the committed CPL, which
  already carries `JLC_CPL_FIX`.
- Agreement means the same rotation and a position within 0.10 mm.
- 348 placed parts: 335 agree, 13 don't.

| Board | Rows | Agree | Disagree |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hub | 146 | 134 | Q1, Q2 (90°); D3, D8, D11–D14, D21–D24 (0.825 mm) |
| Node | 95 | 94 | SW3 (0.17 mm on the pads, 0.30 mm on the pegs) |
| DATA ×4 | 87 | 87 | — |
| DATA port | 6 | 6 | — |
| Camera pod | 14 | 14 | — (it confirms the pod's "to confirm" fixes for D1–D4 and J2) |

**What the suite also flags, though placement is right:**
- **33 land-pattern residuals over its 0.10 mm limit**, at most 0.26 mm (TO-252), with the centres
  agreeing.  These are toe/heel differences between the KiCad and EasyEDA land patterns.  The suite
  wants a package-specific limit with evidence for each.
- **Pin-1 "mismatches" on the USB4105 and the KPA-3010.**  They come from pad names that differ
  between libraries (`A1/B12` vs `1`; LED `1 = K` vs `3 = C`), not from wrong geometry.

## What the suite caught that Konnect and kicad-cli didn't

kicad-cli passes all five boards clean here.  So did the earlier Konnect runs recorded in
`review-konnect.md`: `run_drc`, `run_erc`, the JLC DFM audit and `validate_for_manufacturing`.
The suite's method found:
- **Placement against JLC's library (H1, H2, N1).**  Only the CPL geometry audit compares the CPL
  with the footprint JLC actually places.
  - The hub's PD switch MOSFETs would go on a quarter-turn off, and its 10 side LEDs 0.83 mm off
    their pads.
  - DRC and Konnect's JLC DFM check only see KiCad's own footprints.
  - The audit also re-derived 335 of the repo's placements independently and agreed, including the
    camera pod's corrections still marked "to confirm".
- **Datasheet requirements that a netlist can't express.**
  - H3: the eFuse SHDN pull-down is too weak to keep the rows off without firmware.
  - P1: the LED resistors' rating.
  - X2: back-powering through I²C across two boards.
  - X3: power-on defaults against the node's current limit.
- **Intent vs result.**  DRC enforces the rules as written, so it can't see:
  - X4: netclass patterns that never match hierarchical net names;
  - D1: a power net left at the default width on a board with no netclass;
  - N6: a flow-through claim the copper doesn't keep.
- **Sourcing evidence.**
  - H4: FA01 is no longer on Diodes' list of orderable parts.
  - N11: catalogue MPN text against the listings.
  - X7: the EP land against TI's package.

**What it costs and where it falls short:**
- **The CPL audit needs an adapter.**  It needs KiCad pads in its frame, JLC's EasyEDA pads (rate
  limited: about 45 requests, then HTTP 403) and explicit pad maps.  It maps pads by name, which
  is wrong where libraries number them differently: the KPA-3010 LED, the USB4105 `A1/B12`, the
  tact switch.  Each of those needed a map with evidence.
- **Its 0.10 mm land-pattern limit flags 33 benign toe/heel differences.**
- **The sourcing validator only passes a row with complete evidence:** package, pinout, CAD,
  stock under 14 days, and approval for every alternate.  Only the two small boards pass.
- **The release manifest is bookkeeping:** it can't pass while any gate is open.

## Not covered

- USBLC6 internal pin pairs (ST's site timed out) and the Arducam Mega's own pinout (403): taken
